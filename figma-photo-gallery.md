## What was built
Warm editorial gallery "Teja's Lens" in Figma Make. Hero ("Moments I want to keep."), 3-column masonry grid of 12 real Unsplash photos with captions, locations and dates, working filter chips (All, Campus, Nature, Food, Travel, Portraits), working lightbox with previous/next and close, three collection cards (Campus Life, Texas Outdoors, Food & Coffee), About section, "Made by Teja • Denton, Texas • 2026" footer.

Tested: Nature filter narrowed grid to 3 photos, lightbox opened selected photo with navigation, published site loads.

Version 2 fixed photo-caption mismatches — replaced nine Unsplash images with accurate ones, e.g. a real forest trail for "Quiet on the trail".

Cover: generated title card, not a screenshot of the live gallery.
