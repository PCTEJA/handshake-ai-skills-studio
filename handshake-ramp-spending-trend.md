## Finding (computed from FRED CSVs RAMPAIANTHROPIC and RAMPAIOPENAI, Jan 2023–Oct 2026)
- Anthropic first passed OpenAI in May 2026 (40.6% vs 39.3%) and stayed ahead 6 straight months.
- Oct 2026: Anthropic 44.8% vs OpenAI 40.6% (gap +4.2 points).
- Dec 2025 to Oct 2026: Anthropic 17.8% to 44.8% (+27.0 points, +152%); OpenAI 40.9% to 40.6% (-0.4 points).
- Surge Feb–Apr 2026: Anthropic +5.8, +6.7, +4.5 points; OpenAI's largest 2026 monthly move was +1.1 points (Sep 2026).
- Explanations tested (per submission description): Feb 2026 launches (Claude Opus/Sonnet 4.6, Xcode integration) match the three biggest monthly jumps; flat OpenAI adoption shows firms added Anthropic rather than switching. Limit: Ramp counts paying businesses, not seats or usage.
