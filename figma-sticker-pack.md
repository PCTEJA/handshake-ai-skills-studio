# Buzz the Bee — Sticker Pack (Figma)

Handshake AI Skills Studio × Figma mission "Turn a Sketch into a Sticker Pack" (2026-11-21). Third of the Figma three.

## Published
- Showcase: https://app.joinhandshake.com/ai-showcase/projects/3833206
- Title: Buzz the Bee — Sticker Pack, by Teja Piepur Chakravarthi, University of North Texas, Computer Science
- Figma file: https://www.figma.com/design/Qxurt56ppz9O968SY6YrZ6/Buzz-the-Bee-%E2%80%94-Sticker-Pack

## What was built
Sketched Buzz the bee in Figma Draw with the Pencil tool (oval striped body, round head, two wings, antennae, legs, stinger, smiling face), used "Make an image" to turn the sketch into a clean flat illustration with bold outlines, removed the white background, deleted the rough sketch vectors, and built a 1280x880 "Sticker Pack — Sheet" frame on a honey-yellow (#FFF3B0) background, titled "buzz the bee" with subtitle "six stickers one cheerful bee".

Six stickers: (1) original bee, (2) bee holding a pink flower (AI edit), (3) bee wearing black sunglasses (AI edit), (4) original bee flipped horizontally, (5) flower bee tilted -12 degrees, (6) bee holding a honey pot with dripping honey (AI edit). Each AI variant's background was removed again after editing. Sheet exported as a 1280x880 PNG (676 KB).

Cover: generated 3:2 cover image with the six bee stickers, not the exported Figma sticker sheet.
