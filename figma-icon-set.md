## What was built
Four-icon set in Figma, drawn from ellipses, rectangles, and lines, all with no fill and a consistent 12px black outline. Two 1200x500 frames, "State A — Icons" and "State B — Icons". Each holds the same four named groups: Power (ring + vertical line), Arrow (diagonal shaft + two barbs), Microphone (capsule body, ring, stand, base), Infinity Loop (two overlapping rings), plus bold 36px title "animated icon set" and centered 20px labels.

State B changes: Power line lifts Y 185 to Y 160, Arrow rotates 90 degrees (NE to SE), Microphone capsule lifts Y 190 to Y 170, Infinity loops resize (left 100 to 120, right 100 to 80). Both frames linked by On-click Navigate interactions with Smart Animate, Ease out, 300 ms, both directions. Tested in Figma's player both directions, both worked.

Mission's Figma Agent step was skipped, as the mission states, because the agent needs a Full seat on a paid plan and is not available on Education plans.

Cover: generated 3:2 cover image with the four icons on white, not the exported Figma frame or a screenshot. State A was also exported as a 1200x500 PNG (19 KB).
