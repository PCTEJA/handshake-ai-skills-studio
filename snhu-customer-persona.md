## What was built

Two customer personas built with the mission's three-stage method and evidence discipline:

- **Persona 1 — "First-Time Python Learner" (ZHuddle):** built only from verified real facts — 550+ UNT students use ZHuddle (live counter verified, moved 550 to 552 on a test signup), goal of 2,000 students next semester, first lessons Variables then Loops then Functions, bite-sized lessons, real practice problems, instant feedback, runnable playground (`print("Hello, Zhuddle!")`), quizzes, daily goal, streaks, XP and badges, headline "Learn Python faster, one lesson at a time." Frustrations, trust, barriers, and learner quotes are marked Unknown — no learner evidence exists yet; no demographics, quotes, or statistics invented. Two segment hypotheses (Guided Starter; Practice-First Explorer) labeled untested. Stereotype check 4/4 passed. Stress test ST-01 (30-minute guided project after Functions plus a free competitor): the first three lessons hold, the project is a distinct format to test with checkpoints, and "Built for UNT students" is no longer distinctive.
- **Persona 2 — "Routine Builder" (SipSteady, from the mission's supplied sample data: 12 reviews, 8 forum posts, 4 survey results, n = 1,214):** 71% use it most often after a workout, 63% say taste matters most, 58% drink it on the go, 42% buy it as a meal replacement; verbatim quotes R1, R6, R7, R10, F7; candidate message "Great taste that fits your routine" (to test); hypotheses Post-Workout Regular and Breakfast Backup User. Stereotype check 4/4 passed.
