Key insight

Heavy use despite low high-stakes trust: 55.3% use AI 2+ hours/day, yet only 16.0% say AI handles complex tasks very well and only 6.6% trust it for important decisions. Developers use it constantly for work they can check, not for work they cannot.

Recommendation

Build for verification first, not autonomy: one-click run-locally, diff against codebase context, generated tests/security checks, cited sources. Market on output quality (top switching reason, 25.1%), not on trust us with important decisions.
