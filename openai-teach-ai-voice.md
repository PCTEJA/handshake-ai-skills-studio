## What was built

ChatGPT project "Teja Voice" (signed in as pcteja1028@gmail.com, Teja Plus):

- 3 writing samples grounded only in verified facts (MS CS at UNT, MLOps Engineer at TCS/Cotality, creator of ZHuddle, Python learning platform for UNT students, 550+ students, GitHub PCTEJA) fed to ChatGPT to generate a detailed voice guide (tone, sentence length, structure, words to use/avoid, openings/closings, signature phrases: "Not because X — because Y", "So I built...", "That's the whole point."). Condensed under-600-word version saved as the project's instructions; verified it persisted.
- Tested the guide by drafting the same ZHuddle invitation as an email and a LinkedIn post. Drift check found the email used "I noticed" instead of the sample's "I kept hearing the same thing from new students" and lacked a signature closing; fix rule added ("Report what people told me instead of what I noticed; keep sentences plain and direct; always end on one signature closing line"), email revised, rule appended to the saved instructions.
- Three LinkedIn post options generated; Option 1 (personal story) picked as the final post.
