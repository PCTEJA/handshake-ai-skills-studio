## What was built
A 10-slide Gamma pitch deck for ZHuddle, the Python learning platform Teja built for UNT students (bite-sized lessons, real practice problems, instant feedback). Slides: title, problem, solution, why now, customer, competition, monetization, traction, team, the ask.

Extensions (mission required at least 2, did 5): (1) The Ask slide rewritten for campus pitch competition judges — ask is the competition prize plus a pilot partnership with UNT's CS department, prize funds to marketing, infrastructure, curriculum development, and the first full-time hire, in that order; timeline 2,000 UNT students next semester, 3 other universities the semester after. (2) Invented numbers replaced — "high failure rates" and "zero acquisition cost" softened, unknowns flagged "to confirm" (total intro-CS enrollment, retention/completion rates, premium pricing). (3) Column chart on Customer slide with real figures: Today (live) 550 students vs Goal (next semester) 2,000, axes labeled Students/Stage. (4) Gamma's Agent as pitch judge gave three doubts (no market sizing, unfinished monetization, placeholder traction/ask); all three fixed. (5) Speaker notes on all 10 slides, deck run through in Presenter View with timer (25 seconds click-through).

Cover: generated title card, not a screenshot of the deck, not described as one in the submission. Gamma's current UI routed the Generate step to its Agent-based generate page (generate-v3) even after classic generator was selected; the mission's prompt, format, 10-slide count, outline review and theme were followed as far as the current UI allows.
