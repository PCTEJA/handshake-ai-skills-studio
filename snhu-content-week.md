## What was built

In Gemini (mission specifies Claude, blocked — same as Mission 3), all five stages, no stage skipped:

- **Stage 1 — Source and fact ledger:** ZHuddle, Teja's Python learning platform for UNT students, 550+ students, first lessons Variables → Loops → Functions, small steps, bite-sized lessons; one idea: a variable is a named box that holds a value.
- **Stage 2 — Weekly voice card and Monday–Friday plan.**
- **Stage 3 — Five full drafts:** Monday short written post (136 words); Tuesday 4-slide carousel plus caption; Wednesday 60-second video script; Thursday email newsletter from Teja; Friday blog post (406 words). Plus a claim map tracing every claim to the ledger.
- **Stage 4 — Correction test:** Gemini refused to invent a learning-speed statistic and to add a reader comment, marking both [NEEDS SOURCE]. Correction was by removal/exclusion — neither appears in the final package or the Stage 4 ledger-only expansion draft, documented in the Doc's Stage 4 Correction Note. The claim map flags the brief Loops/Functions descriptions in the Friday blog as general Python definitions, not ZHuddle facts.
- **Stage 5 — Final checks, all six PASS** (purpose/audience, format/length, consistency, ledger-only facts, originality/bias/safety, claim verification).

No student numbers, quotes, course codes, dates, prices, or links invented; no ZHuddle URL invented, so the pieces contain no link.
