## What was built
A 7-slide Gamma deck from Teja's Stack Overflow Developer Survey 2026 analysis. One point: developers will use AI when they can check it. Verified numbers only: 6,872 of 14,304 developers (48%) said trust in AI was conditional on verifiability; 7,432 (52%) did not give that response. Second signal: Ramp AI Index, Anthropic overtook OpenAI in May 2026 (no share quoted). Includes a two-bar chart of 6,872 vs. 7,432, speaker notes on all 7 slides, and a "to confirm" flag for breakdowns by role, experience, or tool. Gamma's Agent summarize-back matched the deck exactly; a speculative sentence was cut.

Cover: rendered title card supplied by Muse, not a screenshot of the deck. The cover also shows "30,903 developers" and "Only 6.6% trust AI for important decisions without checking" — both verified from the same survey analysis (CSV had 30,903 rows; 949 of 14,304 = 6.6% trust AI including important decisions); they are not in the deck itself.
